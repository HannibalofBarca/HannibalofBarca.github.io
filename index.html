About Me

I am a Researcher in the Center for Robotics and Embodied Intelligence at New York University. I hold an MS in Mechatronics and Robotics from NYU and a BS in Mechanical Engineering from BITS Pilani.

My research centers on reinforcement learning and planning, especially for VLA models, and I am currently exploring world models as a foundation for more capable, generalizable robot policies.

My goal is to build robotic systems that can autonomously explore and map unstructured, real-world environments, and that work alongside people to make hazardous or demanding tasks safer and more efficient. I am committed to making sure this technology is developed and deployed responsibly, with careful attention to its ethical and societal impact.
